FAQ

Is SettleClear a substitute for my CPA?
No. SettleClear helps you see why a Stripe deposit is a certain amount and drafts a journal CSV you can map to your chart of accounts. Accuracy is best-effort against Stripe’s reported gross/fee/net — it is not CPA advice, tax advice, or an audit. Always verify totals against your bank deposit before posting.
What’s free vs SettleClear Membership?
Free: one full conversion in this browser (real journal + explanation — not a teaser). Membership: $12/mo for ongoing payouts, cancel anytime on Gumroad. Optional one-file unlock ($4) may be offered when available — primary offer is membership.
Will the journal net match Stripe’s reported deposit?
On the public sample, the deposit nets to $1,096.71 (worked example — not a price). On paid use: if Stripe changes export columns or a journal doesn’t net to Stripe’s reported deposit, we fix the parsers and iterate with you — not a fake money-back guarantee.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel or manage billing on Gumroad anytime. Access continues through the period you already paid; no annual lock-in.
Do you connect to Stripe or store my books?
No Stripe OAuth, no sync, no cloud ledger. Upload a CSV; processing stays in the browser. We are not an AI bookkeeper.
QuickBooks Online and Xero both work?
Yes. Every successful conversion downloads a QBO-friendly journal CSV and a Xero-friendly journal CSV. Map accounts to your COA before posting.
What currencies are supported?
USD-first. Other currencies will convert/parse as numbers and show a warning — review carefully before importing.
